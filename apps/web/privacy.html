Privacy.

The entire product is built so there is almost nothing to disclose. This page states it precisely.

What leaves your device

Nothing you scan. Every message, URL and file you check is processed inside your own browser or the extension's offscreen document. There is no Scamless server, no analytics service, no logging, no account.

What is downloaded

Exactly once, the detection model (~118 MB) is downloaded from the Hugging Face Hub and cached on your device. After that the product works fully offline. Model files contain no personal data.

What is stored locally

Your scan history (last 50 scans) and any "this was wrong" corrections live in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device unless you personally export them using the export button, and even then the upload destination is entirely your choice.

Permissions used (extension)

contextMenus / scripting / activeTab - to check text you explicitly select or press the button for. storage / unlimitedStorage - to cache the model and your local history. offscreen - to run the model in a hidden page. No permission reads your data in the background.

Open source

The full source code is public at github.com/RishavGupta01/Scamless. Anyone can verify every claim on this page.
